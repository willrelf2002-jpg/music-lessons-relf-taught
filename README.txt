# Will Relf Music Website

A complete responsive one-page website for piano/drum lessons and session musician work.

## Files
- index.html — website
- style.css — design and responsive layout
- script.js — small mobile-menu interaction

## Before publishing
1. Replace every `YOUR PHONE NUMBER` / `YOUR PHONE` with your real number.
2. Replace every `YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS` / `YOUR-EMAIL@example.com` with your real email.
3. Replace the photo placeholders with your own photos.
4. Replace the three video cards with your YouTube/Vimeo links.
5. Replace the three sample review texts with genuine reviews from students.
6. For proper online calendar booking, connect the Free Taster section to Calendly, Google Calendar booking, or another booking provider. The current form opens the visitor's email app so it works without a paid service.

The design uses Google Fonts (DM Sans and Manrope), so an internet connection is needed for those fonts; the site otherwise uses no external JavaScript libraries.
